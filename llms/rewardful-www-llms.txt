# Rewardful

> Rewardful is affiliate and referral management software for SaaS. It connects to Stripe or Paddle, automatically tracks referrals and commissions (adjusting for upgrades, downgrades, cancellations, and refunds), and pays affiliates in bulk via PayPal or Wise. First campaigns typically go live in under 15 minutes with no code. Used by 3,000+ SaaS, tech, and AI teams; a Stripe Premier Partner with a 0% transaction fee on all plans.

Last updated: 2026-07-07

## Pricing

All plans: 14-day free trial, 0% transaction fee, unlimited affiliates and visitors, all integrations, multiple currencies, coupon tracking, self-referral fraud detection, first/last-touch attribution, double-sided incentives. Annual billing includes 2 months free.

- [Pricing & Plans](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing)
- Starter — $49/mo: up to $7,500/mo from affiliates. 1 campaign, up to 2 team members, REST API, chat & email support.
- Growth — $99/mo (most popular): up to $15,000/mo. Adds unlimited campaigns and team members, branded affiliate portal, custom domain, private campaigns, custom rewards, custom scripts.
- Enterprise — $149+/mo: over $15,000/mo. Adds one-click PayPal payouts and phone support. Custom plans on request.

## Billing & Policies

- Requires a Stripe or Paddle account (Paddle Classic included; custom Stripe flows via API).
- Cancel anytime from your account; access runs to the end of the billing cycle. No partial refunds mid-cycle. Refunded payments auto-recalculate the affiliate commission.
- Support: hello@rewardful.com — [Cancellation](https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/11623453-how-to-cancel-your-rewardful-subscription) · [Stripe](https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/2213091-how-does-rewardful-use-my-stripe-account) · [Paddle](https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/6664900-how-does-rewardful-use-my-paddle-account) · [All FAQs](https://help.rewardful.com/en/collections/1092743-frequently-asked-questions)
